# 3D Printed Case Designs (Orange/Compact)

This document contains references to 3D design plans and STL models tailored for our motorcycle navigation unit.

## 1. Physical Specifications
- **Dimensions**: ~85mm x 60mm x 20mm (Handheld compact form factor)
- **Material**: PETG or ASA (Recommended for high heat, UV resistance, and motorcycle durability)
- **Color**: Vibrant Orange (Easy visibility and high contrast)

## 2. Model Structure
The case consists of 3 parts:
1. **Front Bezel**: Snap-fit frame keeping the 2.8" IPS TFT and GT911/FT6336 touch panel securely flush.
2. **Main Body**: Enclosure for ESP32-S3 board, INMP441 Mic (pointing forward), MAX98357 Speaker (ported to rear/side), and 1000mAh battery.
3. **Back Plate**: Features an integrated cavity for 4x strong Neodymium magnets ($10\text{mm} \times 2\text{mm}$) or a standard clip rail matching the easy-detach mount.
