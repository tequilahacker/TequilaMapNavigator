# Easy-Detach Mounting Mechanism for Motorcycle Navigation

To make the device handheld-friendly and quickly removable (to prevent theft when parked), use one of the two mounting approaches outlined below.

## Option 1: Magnetic Quick Release (Recommended)
This method uses high-strength Neodymium magnets embedded inside the case back plate, matching a steel plate on the motorcycle handlebar mount.

### Required Materials:
- **Device Side**: 4x Neodymium Disc Magnets ($10\text{mm} \times 3\text{mm}$ - N52 rated) embedded inside the back plate.
- **Mount Side**: Stainless Steel plate or matching magnets (oriented with opposite polarity) mounted onto the handlebar.
- **Safety Tether**: A lightweight paracord tether looped around the handlebar to secure the device in case of high-velocity bumps.

---

## Option 2: Mechanical Slide-In Clip Mount
A traditional wedge-clip locking mechanism similar to bicycle computer mounts (e.g., Garmin style).

### Required Materials:
- **Device Side**: Male quarter-turn or slot-in wedge printed as part of the back cover.
- **Mount Side**: Female bracket clamped to the handlebar tube ($22.2\text{mm}$ or $25.4\text{mm}$ standard handlebar sizes).
- **Release Lever**: Spring-loaded thumb tab on the mount to slide and lock/unlock the unit.
